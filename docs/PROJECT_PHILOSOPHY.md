# DAX Daytrading Bot — Project Philosophy: Fliegen lernen

Status: **BINDING**

Owner: long-term project purpose, family-capital principle, manual Turbo request
and the repository-led development thread. Technical truth, current evidence and
authorization remain with their existing owners.

## Fliegen lernen

We are not building a bot whose purpose is to put real money at risk as quickly
as possible. We are building a market system that must first learn to fly
safely, traceably, robustly and with economic purpose.

A strategy alone is not a dependable trading system. Data truth, broker truth,
state, recovery, reconciliation, evidence, risk, execution safety, decisions and
their causes all belong to the aircraft. Local patches alone create a patchwork.
The deterministic H/D/B/S/O/K Bot-Helper collective is the **flight control**:
it must identify not only that something failed, but why, which failure class it
belongs to and whether REPAIR, REFACTOR, REDESIGN or NO_CHANGE is justified.

Turbo V1.1 is the development-time **afterburner**. It may learn from trades,
losses, NO_TRADES, rejected candidates, filters, regimes, incidents, trials and
external evidence. It may form falsifiable hypotheses, challenge assumptions,
compare alternatives, create isolated challengers and recommend research. It
must never confuse insight with certainty, mutate production autonomously or
acquire Runtime, Risk, Filter, Promotion, Deployment or Execution authority.

The recurring milestone question is:

> Are we better at flying, or did we merely bolt more technology onto the jet?

If additional benefit is not supported, **NO_CHANGE** is a valid good decision.

## DEMO is the flight school

**DEMO IS NOT A TOY MODE. DEMO IS THE FLIGHT SCHOOL.** It is where the system
may later accumulate flight hours under real market physics without unnecessary
real-capital exposure. Good and bad trades, losses, NO_TRADES, rejected
candidates, changing regimes, unusual opportunities, wrong and right
hypotheses, broker/data faults, stale data and technical disruption are all
learning material.

A good day or month is not profitability evidence. Any later LIVE step needs,
at minimum, durable controllable operation; learning/research that demonstrably
improves decisions without overfitting; and positive economic evidence after
realistic costs across adequate time, regimes and independent evidence. No
unvalidated minimum duration, profit target or promotion threshold is created
here.

## Family capital and economic purpose

This project is not a shortcut to getting rich. Its long-term purpose is to help
a five-person family build greater financial stability, dependable additional
income, room to act and financial independence. Capital is not play money.
Financial urgency is real, but it is never a market signal or a reason for
uncontrolled risk.

`FAMILY_CAPITAL_PRINCIPLE=ECONOMIC_RETURN_WITH_CAPITAL_SURVIVAL`

**PROFITABILITY AND CAPITAL SURVIVAL BELONG TOGETHER.** The objective is not the
largest individual win. It is a system that survives long enough, operates
reliably enough and develops a robust enough economic advantage to contribute
to the family over the long term.

Engineering, Safety and Research serve the shared objective
`ROBUST_POSITIVE_ECONOMIC_VALUE_AFTER_REALISTIC_COSTS`. This does not authorize
an unevidenced profitability claim, risk creep, Martingale, revenge trading,
loss chasing, historical over-optimization, “we need money today” reasoning or
technical expansion without measurable benefit.

## Manual Turbo request — request is not override

`TURBO_REQUEST_RIGHT=USER_MAY_REQUEST|SYSTEM_MAY_RECOMMEND|HARD_SAFETY_MAY_VETO`

`TURBO_OVERRIDE_RIGHT=NONE`

A future user request such as “Prüfe Turbo” may start an independent evaluation.
It is not positive market evidence. The possible evaluation results are
TURBO_RECOMMENDED, TURBO_AVAILABLE, NORMAL_ONLY, TURBO_BLOCKED or
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Financial need, recent losses or drawdown may never make
Turbo more likely.

The system may recommend Turbo only when exceptional, causally observable and
previously validated conditions match a separately governed policy. It may not
invent a risk policy. A materially more aggressive future policy remains
possible only after its own Research, OOS/WF, realistic-cost, tail/drawdown,
DEMO/Shadow and independent-G evidence and separate human authorization.

Hard Safety always has veto authority, including stale data, unknown broker
state, inventory contradiction, reconciliation uncertainty, unresolved
transport, unknown protection, hard drawdown/loss limits, margin safety,
account/instrument mismatch, kill switch, hard LIVE block and PTC veto. There is
no revenge Turbo, Martingale Turbo or loss-chasing Turbo.

Current truth is unchanged:

- NORMAL / BOOST / TURBO are **RESEARCH LABELS ONLY**;
- `TURBO_RUNTIME_ACTIVATION=NO`;
- `TURBO_REAL_RISK_CHANGE=NO`;
- `execution_capability=NONE`;
- `order_execution_enabled=false`.

## Canonical development thread

Repository truth carries the project forward:

`REPOSITORY TRUTH → CURRENT_WORK_STEP → MASTERSTAND → EVIDENCE → LESSONS LEDGER → HYPOTHESIS/TRIAL REGISTRIES → FILTER GOVERNANCE → HANDOFF → NEXT WORK`

Each authorized Work run rehydrates the current context from the authoritative
owners. At closeout it writes new evidence, lessons and handoff information back
to those same owners. The main chat remains responsible for architecture,
priority, governance, promotion and Red-Team review; it need not be a permanent
manual data courier.

This is not authorization for a persistent Work daemon, agent network, runtime
Internet agent, self-deployment or unlimited research. Work remains bounded by
each explicit mandate. Existing Turbo Lessons and research registries are reused.

For a concrete unresolved question, an External Scout may publish a
`HIGH_VALUE_RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` containing Problem, Observed Gap, External Idea,
Why It May Help, Evidence, Expected Benefit, Complexity Cost, Research Cost,
Safety Impact, Suggested Cheap First Test and REUSE/ADAPT/REJECT/DEFER. It is a
research proposal only and has no productive authority.

## Binding review principles

Every major Architecture, Research, Filter or Workflow proposal must be checked
against:

- `EVIDENCE_BEFORE_CONFIDENCE`
- `LEARNING_BEFORE_PROMOTION`
- `DEMO_BEFORE_LIVE`
- `ROOT_CAUSE_BEFORE_PATCH`
- `ROBUSTNESS_BEFORE_OPTIMUM`
- `ECONOMIC_VALUE_WITHOUT_UNEVIDENCED_RISK_CREEP`
- `SURVIVAL_BEFORE_EGO`
- `FAMILY_CAPITAL_PRINCIPLE`

This philosophy explains **why** the project exists. It never supersedes current
Code, Evidence, Safety/Governance contracts, `CURRENT_WORK_STEP`, readiness
matrices or explicit authorization.
